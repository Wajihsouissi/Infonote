What’s the strongest argument against launching in September?
3 steps · read 3 cards · 2 searches
9.7s
Drag this section to the canvas
Support capacity, not readiness
The product argument is fine — Beta scope cut is honest about what ships. The real risk is that you are two people and September is when your enterprise pilot renewals land.
A beta at your waitlist size (1,400) typically produces 40–60 tickets in the first fortnight1, concentrated in the first 72 hours.
Your renewal calls sit in the same window, and Pricing model v3 puts 62% of ARR behind them.
If support and renewals collide, you will discount to buy back the time — which is exactly the outcome the new pricing was meant to prevent.
The counter-argument
October pushes you into the November noise floor, where launch attention drops sharply2. If you can staff the first 72 hours, September still wins.
