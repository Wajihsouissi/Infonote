Alternate — task lanes

The week is a board whose columns are days

Instead of showing tasks inside a month grid, Week scale drops the hour grid and becomes seven lanes of tasks. Cards are not placed at all here — they only tint their tasks. This is a planner rather than a diary: you move work between days the way you move it between lanes.

Why it might win

The strongest weekly planner of the three. Nothing competes for the cell — a day is a list of work, full stop — and dragging between days is one obvious gesture. It also reuses the lane drag code the board already has.

What it costs

It replaces the hour grid, so timed events lose their home and multi-day card spans have nowhere to draw. Month view still needs an answer, so you end up designing task display twice.
