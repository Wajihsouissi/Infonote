Alternate — task inspector

The grid stays cards; the rail is the workbench

The month grid is left exactly as it is today — one card, one chip, no task ever drawn in a cell. Selecting a day, a card or a range fills a permanent right rail with the tasks behind it, and every date edit happens there. The calendar answers "what is scheduled"; the rail answers "what do I do about it".

Why it might win

Cheapest by a distance: the grid, the day cell height and the node's mirrored size are all untouched, so nothing about the existing calendar has to be re-tuned. The rail has room for descriptions, images and subtasks that a 200 px cell never will.

What it costs

You cannot see the month's workload at a glance — the calendar still only shows containers, so "which week is overloaded" needs a click per day. It also eats the unscheduled tray's 288 px, or fights it for the space.
