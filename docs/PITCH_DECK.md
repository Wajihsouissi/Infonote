## Slide 2 — The Problem
**Notes are trapped in lists.**

- Traditional note apps force your thinking into top-to-bottom documents and folders.
- Ideas are connected — but your tools keep them in separate, flat pages.
- You switch between a docs app, a task board, and a whiteboard just to plan one project.
- Result: context is scattered, and the big picture is lost.
- **Visual:** Cramped stack of folders/documents on the left vs. an open canvas on the right.

---

## Slide 3 — The Solution
**One infinite canvas for everything.**

- Chnk it puts your notes, tasks, and media on a single 2D space you can pan and zoom forever.
- Connect cards visually to map how ideas actually relate.
- Write rich content, manage tasks, and generate with AI — without ever leaving the canvas.
- **Visual:** Hero shot of the canvas with note cards, a Kanban board node, and connection lines.

---

## Slide 4 — How It Works
**Think spatially. Work in blocks.**

1. **Drop a node** anywhere — a note, a content block, or a Kanban board.
2. **Write in blocks** — headings, lists, quotes, images, columns, embeds (Notion-style block editor).
3. **Connect & arrange** — drag, snap to grid, link cards to build a visual map.
4. **Zoom out** to see the whole project; zoom in to focus.
- **Visual:** 4-step numbered flow with small icons.

---

## Slide 5 — Key Features
**A whole workspace, not just a notebook.**

- 🗺️ **Infinite canvas** — pan, zoom, minimap, snap-to-grid
- ✍️ **Block editor** — rich text, columns, media, slash commands
- ✅ **Kanban boards** — tasks with status, priority, assignees, due dates
- 🤖 **AI built in** — generate text, images, and whole sets of cards from a prompt
- ☁️ **Cloud sync & collaboration** — work together, synced in real time
- 📄 **PDF & media** — drop in documents and files
- **Visual:** 2×3 feature grid with icons.
