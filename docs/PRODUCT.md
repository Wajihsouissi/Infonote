# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React, Vite, CSS Modules

## Users

Thinkers, researchers, and creators who need an unconstrained space (infinite canvas) to brainstorm, take notes, and connect ideas locally.

## Product Purpose

Provides a local-first, infinite canvas with a block editor that allows scattering cards fast and connecting them later, without forcing upfront structure.

## Positioning

An infinite canvas and text editor combined, saving local-first.

## Operating Context

Used by individuals at their desk (desktop web) wanting to visually lay out their thoughts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Works offline / local-first with cloud sync.
- Block editor with slash menu & markdown.
- AI text generation.

## Brand Commitments

- "Paper & Ink" design aesthetic (flat editorial surfaces, hairline rules, electric persimmon accent).
- No glassmorphism, no cheap glows.

## Product Principles

1. Speed to thought: dropping a card should be instant.
2. Structure comes later: scatter first, organize later.
3. Your data is yours: local-first by default.
